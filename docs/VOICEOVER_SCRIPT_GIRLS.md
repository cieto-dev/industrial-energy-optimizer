# URJIVA: Two-Person Voiceover Demo Script

**Context for the Presenters:**
This script is designed for two people (Presenter 1 & Presenter 2) to dynamically talk through the URJIVA product demo.
**To start:** Go to `http://localhost:3000/assessment`. Click the small **"Load UP Demo"** button at the top right to instantly auto-fill all correct values. Do not type them out manually.

---

### 🎬 [0:00] The Landing & Factory Profile
*(Start recording on the Assessment page with fields empty)*

**Presenter 1:** 
"Welcome to URJIVA. This is our operational decision-support engine designed specifically for plant managers."

**Presenter 2:** 
"Today, we're evaluating a mid-sized textile plant in Uttar Pradesh. Instead of typing everything manually, we'll hit our 'Load Demo' preset to fill in the baseline."

*(Action: Click "Load UP Demo". The fields populate instantly.)*

**Presenter 1:** 
"As you can see, the plant requires 160°C process heat and currently burns 2,000 kg of coal a day. But notice one critical detail in Step 3—we are leaving the CAPEX budget completely blank."

*(Action: Scroll to Step 3 to show the blank CAPEX field, then click 'Run Optimization Engine')*

---

### 🎬 [0:30] The Blocked State & Technical Feasibility
*(Action: The Results page loads, showing the Amber 'Blocked' State.)*

**Presenter 2:** 
"This is the URJIVA 'No-Invention Rule' in action. Because we lack a firm CAPEX budget, the engine intentionally halts the financial recommendation. We refuse to let AI hallucinate costs."

**Presenter 1:** 
"Exactly. But underneath those warnings, the engine still ran the thermodynamic models. If you scroll down, you'll see the Preliminary Technical Rankings. It has identified Biomass and Solar as structurally viable, ensuring they meet the 160°C requirement without risking production downtime."

*(Action: Scroll down to show the Biomass Boiler pathway, highlighting the 'Vendor Quote Required' tags)*

---

### 🎬 [1:15] The Visualizations Tab
*(Action: Scroll back to the top of the Results page and click the "Visualizations" tab)*

**Presenter 2:** 
"While we wait for a vendor quote to unlock the financial metrics, plant managers still need to visualize the technical impact. Let's switch to the Visualizations tab."

*(Action: Click the 'Energy Flow & Sankey' inner tab)*

**Presenter 1:** 
"Here in the Energy Flow tab, we map out the exact thermal transition. You can see how the energy shifts from 100% coal input to a hybrid mix of Biomass Residue and Rooftop Solar, mapping the exact conversion efficiencies."

*(Action: Click the '10-Year Cumulative Trajectory' inner tab)*

**Presenter 2:** 
"And if we look at the trajectory, the engine honestly blocks the Cash Flow projections because of the missing CAPEX. However, it still accurately projects the Scope 1 emission reductions over the next decade."

---

### 🎬 [2:00] Report Generation & Closing
*(Action: Click the 'Full Report' button at the top)*

**Presenter 1:** 
"Finally, the plant manager can export this exact view. Our report generation previews the methodology, the explicitly flagged data gaps, and our strict No-Invention disclaimer."

**Presenter 2:** 
"This ensures absolute transparency when taking this roadmap to management or a bank. Once real CAPEX data is ingested, these ranges collapse into firm payback figures—unlocking the final investment decision."

**Presenter 1:** 
"This is URJIVA. Decarbonization without risking production."

*(End recording)*
