# Demo Script

**[0:00 - 0:45] Assessment & Smart Defaults:**
"Welcome to URJIVA. This is the operational interface used by plant engineers. We're running a textile plant in Tamil Nadu. Notice that when we select 'Textile', the system smartly pre-fills the energy baseline—like coal usage and 160°C process temps—clearly marked as 'DEFAULT'. We'll leave the CAPEX budget blank to see how the engine handles uncertainty."

**[0:45 - 1:45] The Blocked State & Technical Feasibility:**
"Because we lack a firm CAPEX budget and the specific equipment cost isn't in our verified knowledge base, the engine halts the financial recommendation. This is our 'No-Invention Rule' in action—we never fake numbers. However, beneath the data gap warnings, the engine still provides Preliminary Technical Rankings based purely on thermodynamics and reliability."

**[1:45 - 2:30] Pathway Detail:**
"Clicking into the top technical pathway, the plant manager sees exactly *why* it was chosen—specifically its temperature match and production neutrality. In the Financial Reality Check, payback and savings are honestly disabled, instructing the user to get a vendor quote."

**[2:30 - 3:00] Report Generation:**
"Finally, the plant manager can export this exact view. Our report generation previews the methodology, the explicitly flagged data gaps, and our No-Invention disclaimer, ensuring absolute transparency when they take this to management or a bank."

**[3:00 - 3:15] Closing:**
"Once real CAPEX data is ingested or entered, the ranges will automatically collapse into firm payback and NPV figures."
