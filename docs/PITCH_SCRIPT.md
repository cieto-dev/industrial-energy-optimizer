# End-to-End Demo Pitch Script
**Target Duration:** 5 minutes
**Persona:** Balanced (Technical robust + High Business/Policy Value)

---

## 1. Introduction (0:00 - 1:00)
**Visual:** Landing Page / Home Screen

"Hello everyone. We are presenting the Industrial Energy Optimizer—an AI-driven decision-support tool built specifically for industrial MSMEs to transition to cleaner energy efficiently.

Today, Indian MSMEs consume over 25% of the industrial sector's energy, largely from high-emission fossil fuels like coal. Transitioning is hard: there are technical constraints, complex financial math, and navigating a labyrinth of government subsidies. 

Our platform solves this by taking simple factory inputs and running them through a multi-engine pipeline:
1. A **Technology Assessment Engine** to filter out impossible solutions.
2. An **Emissions & Economics Engine** to calculate CAPEX, OPEX, and Paybacks.
3. A **Policy Engine** to automatically match eligible subsidies.
4. And finally, an **Optimizer** utilizing Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis (MCDA) to rank the best pathways."

---

## 2. Factory Input Walkthrough (1:00 - 2:30)
**Visual:** Assessment Form (`/assessment`)

"Let’s see it in action. We are a medium-scale Textile factory in Tamil Nadu, operating 300 days a year. We currently use a coal boiler for our 200°C process heating, consuming about 10 tonnes of coal a day.

*Action: Fill out the form fields for General, Production, and Technical tabs.*

One of the biggest blockers for MSMEs is capital. So, in the **Eligibility** tab, we input our Udyam registration details and our Special Category status—in this case, 'Women Owned'. 

*Action: Select 'Women Owned' checkbox and submit the form.*

When I click submit, the backend doesn't just do simple math. It generates combinatorial scenarios (e.g., Solar Thermal, Biomass, Electrification), runs constraints checks against the Textile industry profile, queries our vectorized policy knowledge base, and performs Monte Carlo risk simulations on payback periods."

---

## 3. The Recommendation Dashboard (2:30 - 4:00)
**Visual:** Dashboard (`/dashboard`)

"Here is our AI-generated Recommendation Dashboard. 

Right at the top, the engine recommends **Biomass Boiler + Heat Recovery**. Why? The explanation box tells us exactly why: it provides a massive 65% CO2 reduction while remaining economically viable with a 3.2-year payback.

But what about the cost? Under **Policy Benefit**, our Policy Engine identified exactly which government schemes we qualify for based on our 'Women Owned' status and our location in Tamil Nadu, reducing our effective CAPEX significantly.

*Action: Scroll down to Scenario Comparison.*

We also provide transparency. The Scenario Comparison table shows how other options fared. For instance, Electrification scored well on emissions, but was ranked lower. 

*Action: Scroll down to Rejection Log.*

Our 'Why not X?' log explains precisely that Electrification was rejected due to its extremely high operating costs and grid reliability constraints in that specific district."

---

## 4. Reporting & Conclusion (4:00 - 5:00)
**Visual:** Reports Page (`/reports`)

"Finally, MSME owners need actionable documents to take to banks or boards. 

*Action: Navigate to Reports, click Download PDF/Excel.*

With one click, we generate a comprehensive, boardroom-ready PDF containing the full financial sensitivity analysis, and an Excel sheet with raw modeling data. 

In conclusion, our Industrial Energy Optimizer bridges the gap between complex climate technology and actionable MSME investment, accelerating industrial decarbonization at scale. Thank you."
