# Near-term Research & Expansion Track

**Target:** Continuous-process industries (specifically Cement and Sponge Iron).

*Note: Thermal power plant coal replacement remains an isolated research track for the next 12–18 months and will not enter the product pipeline.*

## Concrete Research Questions (Next 4-6 weeks)

1. What are the validated IPCC emission factors for alternate fuels (e.g., RDF, municipal solid waste) specifically in high-temp kiln operations?
2. What are the standard spatial/land density constraints (sqm/kW) typically faced by Indian cement plants for onsite solar/wind integration?
3. Can we map the exact BEE PAT cycle 3 normalization baseline benchmarks for sponge iron kilns?
4. What are the historically documented grid reliability metrics (uptime %) for heavy industrial corridors in Chhattisgarh and Odisha?
