# AI-Powered Industrial Energy Transition Platform

> Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2026

An AI-assisted decision support platform that helps Indian Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) identify technically feasible, economically viable, and environmentally sustainable industrial decarbonization pathways.

The platform combines engineering knowledge, industrial process analysis, optimization techniques, and artificial intelligence to recommend the best transition strategy for industries aiming to reduce energy costs and carbon emissions.

---

# Problem Statement

Industrial decarbonization is one of the biggest challenges facing India's manufacturing sector. Many MSMEs continue to depend on conventional fossil-fuel-based systems due to limited technical expertise, financial uncertainty, and lack of access to decision-support tools.

Existing solutions often:

- Focus only on emissions.
- Ignore technical feasibility.
- Do not compare multiple transition pathways.
- Lack transparency in recommendations.
- Require expert consultation.

This project aims to bridge that gap by providing an intelligent, explainable, and research-backed recommendation platform.

---

# Project Objectives

The platform is designed to:

- Analyse existing industrial energy systems.
- Understand process-specific energy requirements.
- Evaluate multiple clean energy technologies.
- Apply engineering and operational constraints.
- Compare transition scenarios.
- Estimate financial viability.
- Quantify emission reductions.
- Recommend the most suitable decarbonization pathway.
- Explain every recommendation in a transparent manner.
